## What's new in 1.3

### My apps: Google, Microsoft, Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn and TikTok

A new **My apps** screen (Me → My apps), with each app's status at a glance.

- **Google**: Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets and Docs. Carino can find, read, draft and send email; see and add events; read files; fill in spreadsheets; and create documents.
- **Microsoft**: Outlook, Calendar and OneDrive, and it writes **Word**, **Excel** and **PowerPoint** files.
- **Instagram and Facebook Pages**: see posts and comments, reply, and post photos.
- **X and LinkedIn**: post as you.
- **TikTok**: send a video to your drafts.
- **Several accounts**: connect a second Google or Microsoft account and Carino picks the one you name.
- **Sending email and posting always wait for you to tap Approve.**
- **Direct sign-in**: Carino signs in from your phone with your own free developer app for each service, so nothing goes through anyone else's server. Each app's page walks you through the one-time setup.

### WhatsApp, like Toqi

- Chat with Carino on WhatsApp: its own number, through Meta's official platform.
- **Create my relay**: paste one Cloudflare token and Carino sets up the free relay that holds messages for your phone.
- **Answer in the background** keeps Carino replying while you use other apps.

### Also

- Videos you attach are kept (up to 50 MB), so Carino can pass them on (e.g. to TikTok).
- "Sign in with the app" for connected apps (MCP) now works on the phone too.
