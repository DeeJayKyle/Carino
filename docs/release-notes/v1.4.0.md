## What's new in 1.4

### One-tap sign-in

In **Me → My apps**, tap **Sign in** and approve on the app's own page. That's it: no developer setup.

- **Works now for:** Google (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Docs), Microsoft (Outlook, Calendar, OneDrive, Word, Excel, PowerPoint), Facebook Pages, Instagram, X and TikTok.
- **LinkedIn** still uses the manual setup for now.
- **Your sign-ins stay on your phone.** Carino's sign-in helper only adds Carino's app key when you sign in or renew; it keeps nothing.
- **Who can sign in (until each company reviews Carino):**
  - **Google:** shows an "unverified app" warning first; tap *Advanced → Go to Carino*.
  - **Facebook, Instagram and TikTok:** only the people added as testers.
- **Your own developer apps still work:** they're under *Advanced* on each app's page.

### WhatsApp, exactly like Toqi

- Carino has its own WhatsApp number. In **Messaging**, tap **Link my WhatsApp**, then send the 6-digit code from WhatsApp (the **Open WhatsApp** button types it for you). You're linked.
- Chat with text, voice notes and photos. Anything that needs your OK comes with **Approve / Decline** buttons.
- Each phone only ever gets its own number's messages, and Carino only replies to your number.
- **Using your own WhatsApp Business number** is still possible, under *Advanced*.
