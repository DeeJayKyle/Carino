## What's new in 1.5.0

### Everyday
- **Smarter reminders.** Reminders can repeat, and the notification has Snooze and Done buttons. You can also be reminded when you arrive at a place.
- **Today widget.** Today's to-dos and your next reminder, on your home screen.
- **Money.** Log spending in chat or from a receipt photo, set monthly budgets, and edit past expenses. Keep personal and pharmacy money in separate books. Track income, recurring bills, debts and savings goals. Your existing records, currencies and receipts are kept as they were.
- **Application tracker.** Follow job and scholarship applications: eligibility, deadlines, documents, referees and follow-ups.
- **Email and calendar.** A daily summary of unread mail, replies you're waiting on and today's events, with to-dos taken from emails and reply drafts. Carino always asks before sending anything or changing your calendar.
- **Routine builder.** Build routines from steps, and choose what each one may use. Anything big still waits for your OK.
- **Voice.** Clear microphone on/off, spoken commands ("pause", "repeat that", "read my brief", "stop") and an optional spoken morning brief.
- **Memory.** Keep personal and work memories apart, delete several at once, and mark a memory "on my devices only".

### AI
- **Offline AI.** A small AI can run on the phone itself, with no internet and no account.
- **AI & connections.** Use several AI services side by side, plus AI on your own devices: Carino finds Ollama or LM Studio on your PC (it only looks on this device and your paired PC). Each model is tested for real (replies, tools, reading photos, speed). Carino then picks the best allowed model for each message, and you can choose one per job.
- **Private by design.** Mark a chat, folder or memory as private and only AI on your devices will see it. Once private content is used in a chat, that chat stays private.
- **Costs under control.** Paid services (such as OpenAI, through its Responses API) are used only after you approve them. You can set daily and monthly spending limits; at a limit, Carino keeps going on free and local AI.

### Your data
- **Encrypted backup and restore.** Backups are protected by a password and checked for damage, and you can preview and test a restore before doing it. Cloud backup to Google Drive or OneDrive is off unless you turn it on.
- **Keys stay locked.** Saved AI keys are encrypted with your phone's secure key store (Android Keystore).
- **Files.** Let Carino read folders you choose: search inside documents, spreadsheets and PDFs, find duplicates and get tidy-up ideas. Moves and deletes always ask first and can be undone.
- **What Carino can reach.** One page lists every folder, account, tool, device and chat Carino can access, each with Remove. Disconnecting Google or Facebook also cancels Carino's access on their side.

### Carino for Windows (new)
- Pair your phone and PC with a QR code and a check number, and keep them in step over your Wi-Fi. If both change the same thing, Carino shows you both versions instead of overwriting either.
- Carry on a chat or task on the other device, open apps you've approved, and work with folders on your PC.

### Fixes
- WhatsApp no longer answers a message twice or loses one when the connection drops.
- Editing a to-do or expense on two devices at once no longer silently overwrites a change.
- Android's own backup no longer copies Carino's private data and keys.
