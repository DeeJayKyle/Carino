## What's new in 1.6.0

### A new Carino, built in Flutter
- **Every screen rebuilt.** Home, chats, to-dos, money, applications, files, routines, memory, settings, devices, apps, messaging and AI & connections are now fast native screens on both Android and Windows. Your data, settings and saved sign-ins carry over: install this over the earlier version.
- **Chat.** Attach photos and files, dictate with your phone's own speech recognition, or talk hands-free in voice mode.
- **Appearance.** Light, dark or system theme, the Midnight palette, larger text and reduced motion now apply instantly.
- **First-run setup** and **Ramble it out** (talk or type everything on your mind, Carino sorts it) are rebuilt too.

### Carino for Windows
- The Windows app is the same new Flutter app, with an installer that needs no administrator rights.
- Pair your phone with the QR code in Settings → Devices, choose folders and apps Carino may use, and find Ollama or LM Studio on your PC, as before.
- If you had the earlier Windows app installed, you can uninstall it after installing this one; your data stays.

### Notes
- Sharing to Carino from another app still uses the earlier screen for now.
- Carino for Windows needs Microsoft Edge WebView2, which Windows 11 and up-to-date Windows 10 already have.
