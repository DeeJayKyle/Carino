
## What's new in 1.1

### Free AI models, chosen for you

- **Only free services, best first:** Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral, NVIDIA, Z.ai (GLM), Ollama Cloud, and Ollama on your PC. Claude, which is paid, is no longer offered (an existing Claude setup keeps working).
- **A model dropdown for each service**, with its top free models in priority order: for example Gemini 3.8 Flash, GPT-OSS 120B and Qwen 3.8 on Groq, `openrouter/free` (auto) and Gemma 4 on OpenRouter, and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash on NVIDIA and Ollama Cloud. DeepSeek's own API has no free tier, so DeepSeek is offered through services that serve it free.
- **Check my key** asks the service which models your key can use, marks each one, and moves to the best one that works. For OpenRouter it also shows any other free models available right now; for Ollama on your PC it lists the models you have installed.
- **Automatic switching:** if a model is retired (404), busy (503) or over its free limit (429), Carino moves on to the next free model in the list by itself, and the error says which models it tried.

These fixes come from the errors seen in setup: Groq removed Llama from its free tier, Google moved new keys to Gemini 3.x, and Gemini was briefly overloaded.

### Smoother to use

- **Every tap answers back.** Buttons, cards, chips and tabs show a soft press tint and a slight press-in.
- **Smooth page changes.** A quick fade between screens; dialogs grow in, or slide up as a sheet on phones.
- **Settings fields sit full-width under their labels** on phones, and disabled buttons look "not ready yet" rather than broken.
- **Easier to read.** Every text colour is checked against WCAG contrast in all four themes (using Adobe Leonardo); warning text was darkened to pass.
- **Still fast.** All motion is plain CSS and native View Transitions, with no animation library, and everything respects "Reduce motion". Carino looks the same as before.

### Fixes found in testing

- **Changes are saved straight away.** Closing the app within a second or two of a change could lose it; Carino now saves within a fraction of a second.

### Tested before release

Every feature was driven through the app's own screens in a phone-sized browser (34 checks), and the real APK is now tested on an Android emulator on every change: setup, streaming chat, to-dos, a notification, a reminder ringing while the app is closed, data kept after a force-stop, sharing into the app, saving to Downloads, text-to-speech, the back button and the widget.
