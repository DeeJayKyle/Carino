## What's new in 1.7.0

### Chat that does more
- **Question cards** with answers to tap, **editable drafts** before anything is sent, and a live "working on it" indicator.
- **Make things in chat:** slides, documents, pictures, short videos, small pages and apps (invites, menus, checklists, quizzes), and WhatsApp sticker packs from your own pictures.
- **Agent abilities:** saved skills, search across earlier chats, a research helper, a safe code sandbox and a browser that can read and fill in pages (anything that sends or buys asks first).

### Smarter AI
- **Picks the right model for each job** (everyday, writing, code, research, pictures) and **switches by itself** when a service fails, is busy or reaches its free limit.
- **Teamwork:** for bigger questions several models work in parallel and one checks the others' answer.
- **Live voice:** talk in real time through OpenAI or Google Gemini, if you connected one (Settings → Voice, or the button in a chat).

### Getting things done
- **AI phone calls:** ask Carino to ring a business (opening hours, a table, a price) through your own Twilio account. Each call waits for your approval and starts by saying it's an AI calling for you; the answers come back in the chat.
- **More apps:** Slack, Todoist, Notion, Linear and GitHub (paste a personal token), and TickTick, Withings and Sonos (sign in with your own developer app).
- **Bank statements:** attach a CSV or Excel statement from any bank or mobile money and add it to Money; importing twice adds nothing twice.
- **Food and travel:** ready links for delivery apps in your country, flights, stays and getting around (you order and book there).
- **Sharing:** split costs fairly, get ready-to-send messages and plans, catch up on a linked group ("what did I miss?"), invite a friend and send feedback.
- **200 routine ideas**, goals on routines, personality presets and "sound like me".

### Your language
- Carino now speaks **English, Português (Brasil), Deutsch, Français, Español, Nederlands, Italiano and Polski**. Choose in Settings → Language & region; "Automatic" follows your phone.

### Updates
- **Carino updates itself** from its official releases (Settings → Updates). Each download is checked before it's installed, and your data is kept.

### Notes
- Live voice, phone calls and some apps need your own account with that service; Carino never shares your keys.
- Install this over 1.6.0; your data and sign-ins carry over.
